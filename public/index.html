Seven primitives. Every agent is built from them.

These are not features. They are the building blocks. Existing agents are made of them, and so is the next one we build for your operation.

C/01Real-time interactionSub-second spoken and written dialogue over telephony, web and messaging. One interaction layer, every channel.
C/02Persistent stateOperational context that survives days, handoffs and channel switches. An agent that remembers is infrastructure; one that forgets is a demo.
C/03Multilingual by defaultLocale-aware dialogue across European and MENA languages, derived from context rather than a dropdown.

C/07Integration & deploymentTwo-way integration with CRM, telephony and enterprise data. Runs in the cloud or on your own infrastructure.
