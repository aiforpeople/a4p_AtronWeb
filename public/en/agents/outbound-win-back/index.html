ATRON / Agent · 03 · Outbound

An agent that calls cold leads and writes the outcome to the CRM.

It calls your lost or cold lead lists, re-measures interest, hands interested leads to your team and writes the outcome of every call with your CRM statuses. You start the campaign; the agent works inside the calling window you set.

Calling live in eleven languages for an enterprise health-tourism operator.

What it does

Calls

Calls each contact in the language of their list and in their own time zone.

Measures

Re-measures interest and separates interested, call-back and not-interested contacts.

Writes

Writes the outcome of every call with the exact statuses in your CRM.

Retries

Retries unreached contacts as many times as you set, and never calls people who opted out.

How it connects

It takes your contact lists and writes outcomes to your CRM (for example Zoho). Campaigns are split by language and country automatically and never start without your approval.

FAQFAQ

Short answers.

Which languages does it call in?

It calls live in Portuguese, Bulgarian, Italian, Romanian, Spanish, Serbian, French, Russian, Dutch, German and English.

What happens to people who don't want calls?

Anyone who says they don't want to be called goes on the do-not-call list and is never called again.

Who starts a campaign?

You do. The agent prepares draft campaigns; no calls start without your approval.

Which process wears you out the most?

Tell us about that process. Half an hour on Google Meet, and a week later you see it running.
