ATRON / Agent · 01 · Voice

A voice agent that answers, qualifies and routes the call.

The ATRON voice agent answers inbound calls, understands what the caller wants, collects the details you need and routes the call to the right person or system. Every call lands in your CRM as a structured record.

Running in production at an enterprise health-tourism operator.

What it does

Answers

Picks up without a queue and speaks the caller's language. No phone menus.

Qualifies

Asks the questions you set; it never fills a blank with a guess.

Records

Writes the outcome and the details it collected to your CRM.

Hands over

Routes the call to your team when a question is left open or the caller asks for a person.

How it connects

For Turkish numbers it works through SIP operators such as Verimor or Netgsm, or through your own cloud PBX. On the CRM side it follows your existing record structure.
